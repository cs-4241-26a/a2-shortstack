Workout Tracker

I made a way to track your workouts and exercises on a website. The user is prompted to give their information about their workout, and then is returned a structured response of the total reps they did with how much calories they burned(which are both derived fields). Using CSS, I was able to emphasize the important information like the calories burned and exercise and I also used a google font to make it look clean. I used display: flex for the form in the CSS and aligned it to the center. 

## Technical Achievements
- **Tech Achievement 1**: 
I did the single-page app achievement where the user is able to submit their exercises, reps and sets, and returned their total reps and calories burned. The total reps and calories burned are both updated derived fields from the users inputs. 

### Design/Evaluation Achievements
- **Design Achievement 1**: 
Tested user interface with student Murcia. He liked the design and how easy it was to use. The one problem he had was with how limiting it was, in that it didn't have a way to upload a schedule, track your weeks, etc. He didn't really make a comment that suprised me, he just told me some changes to make like creating a schedule, a dropdown select for the particular exercises that you can do rather thna typing it out, etc.

Second person I did this with was McGowen. He said that the calories burned was a little inaccurate, and that every exercise burning the same amount of calories was not accurate. He mainly focused on me changing the logic of my code which was definitely insightful to me.
